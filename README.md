## Documentation

Web3 Quant Hackathon - Team 74 100BTC

**Strategy:**
Our team use Chandelier Exit (CE) as our primary indicator, ATR(10) Supertrend as a short term trend confirmation indicator for our trading bot. We choose 15 min timeframe candle as our trading environment. When a Long direction is generated by CE and Uptrend signal is generated by ATR(10), enter long. When a Short direction is generated by CE || P/L hit Take Profit 5% || P/L hit Stop Loss 2.5%, exit current trade.

CE: (Period = 22, Mutiplier = 3.5)
1. Look back Period candle -> find the highest high
2. Long Stop = Highest High - (Multiplier x ATR)
3. Short Stop = Lowest Low - (Mutiplier x ATR)
4. Rachet: Long Stop can only move up, never down
5. Direction = Long, when Close > Short Stop
6. Flip to Short only when Close < Long Stop
7. Otherwise hold previous direction

ATR (10) Supertrend: (Period = 10, Mutiplier = 3)
1. Short term trend confirmation
2. Avoid enter long during down trend

Further improvement:
A shorter term ATR can be used, for example: period = 2, multi = 1. It can provide a sign for quicker exit during trade. Solve the problem of late exit as CE Short direction require great draw down in candle which loss a lot of potential gain.
